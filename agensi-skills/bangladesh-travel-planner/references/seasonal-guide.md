# Seasonal Travel Guide — Bangladesh

## Quick Decision Matrix

| Destination Type | Best Months | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General sightseeing** | Nov – Feb | Jun – Sep | Dry, comfortable 20-28°C |
| **Sundarbans** | Nov – Feb | Apr – Oct | Cyclone risk, monsoon flooding |
| **Haor wetlands** | Jul – Oct | Dec – Mar | Need high water for boats |
| **Hill Tracts** | Oct – Mar | Jun – Sep | Landslide and flash flood risk |
| **Tea gardens** | Nov – Mar | — | Year-round, but clearest views |
| **Cox's Bazar beach** | Nov – Mar | Jun – Sep | Rough seas, monsoon |
| **River cruises** | Oct – Feb | — | Water levels stable |
| **Heritage / urban** | Nov – Feb | — | Year-round, but cooler is better |

## Monsoon Impact by Division

| Division | Flood Risk | Road Risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sylhet | Very High | High | Haor overflow, flash floods |
| Mymensingh | High | Medium | Brahmaputra flooding |
| Rangpur | Medium | Medium | Teesta flooding |
| Barishal | High | High | River islands submerge |
| Dhaka | Medium | Low | Urban waterlogging only |
| Chattogram | High (hills) | Very High | Landslides in hill tracts |
| Rajshahi | Low | Low | Driest division |
| Khulna | High (coastal) | Medium | Cyclone exposure |

## National Holidays (Approximate Dates)

Peak domestic travel around:
- **Eid ul-Fitr** and **Eid ul-Adha** (dates shift yearly per lunar calendar)
- **Pohela Boishakh** (Bengali New Year, April 14)
- **Victory Day** (December 16)
- **Independence Day** (March 26)

Expect heavy crowding and inflated transport prices during Eid holidays.
